What I'm looking for

PhD positions

I’m interested in PhD opportunities in machine learning, model reliability, biomedical AI, and research software engineering. I’m particularly drawn to research groups working on reliable AI, computational neuroscience, and reproducible methods for biomedical data across Europe, Australia, and Canada. I want to be in places where getting the answer right matters as much as getting the code to run.
