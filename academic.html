Academic Portfolio

Research, with an emphasis on reliability

I build machine learning systems for biomedical and clinical data, along with the software needed to make their results reproducible. My work spans clinical prediction, computational genomics, spatial transcriptomics, and the evaluation of model reliability.

My academic path began in chemistry and later expanded into computational neuroscience. Moving between these fields has shaped how I approach computational research: models are tools to investigate, test, and question — not answers to accept at face value.

Research Experience

Computational Pipeline & Neuro Representation Analysis Lead

NeuroTeem, 2026

Project: Exposure-dependent evolution and stabilisation of neural representations under repeated experience

  • · Built a modular analysis pipeline for population-level neural activity recorded under repeated visual experience and varying metabolic conditions, using the Allen Brain Observatory Visual Coding dataset.
  • · Implemented reusable workflows covering data ingestion, quality control, dimensionality reduction, representational distance measures, and characterisation of neural trajectories.
  • · Added statistical modules applying Spearman correlation, permutation testing, bootstrap validation, and false-discovery-rate correction.
  • · Integrated contributions from three other researchers into a single coherent framework, with version control, documented parameters, and automated integrity checks.
